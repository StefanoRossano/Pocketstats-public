Pocket Stats - Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

1. Introduction

Pocket Stats ("the App") is a statistics and tracking application for Pokémon TCG Pocket gameplay.

This Privacy Policy explains how Pocket Stats handles user data. The App is designed to operate locally on the user's device and does not operate a developer-owned server or require users to create an account.

2. Data Collection

Pocket Stats does not collect, sell, or store user data on servers operated by the developer.

The App does not require users to provide names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise location, contacts, advertising identifiers, or similar personal information to the developer.

The Tournaments feature may require the user to provide a Limitless username. This username is used to identify the user's public tournament history on Limitless and to retrieve tournament data through Limitless services. The username and the retrieved tournament data are stored locally on the user's device.

3. Data Stored on the Device

Information entered or imported by the user, such as decks, matches, seasons, statistics, tournament data, Limitless username, decklists, and related game-tracking information, is stored locally on the user's device.

This information is used solely to provide the functionality of the App.

4. Images

The App may allow the user to select images from the device for use within the App. These images are selected by the user and are used locally by the App.

Pocket Stats does not upload these user-selected images to a server operated by the developer.

The Tournaments feature may also download publicly available card images from the Limitless CDN in order to generate decklist images locally on the device. These images are not uploaded to a server operated by the developer.

5. Limitless Integration

The Tournaments feature integrates with services provided by Limitless to verify a user's Limitless username and retrieve publicly available tournament information associated with that username.

When the Tournaments feature is used, the App may send the Limitless username entered by the user to Limitless services and receive tournament and decklist information in response.

This communication is directly between the App and Limitless services. The developer of Pocket Stats does not receive or store this information on a developer-operated server.

Limitless is an independent third-party service. Its handling of data is governed by its own privacy policy and terms.

6. Backup and Device Transfer

Pocket Stats stores its application data locally. Depending on the user's Android device and Google account settings, Android system backup and device-transfer features may back up or restore application data.

Such backup and restoration are controlled by the Android operating system and the user's device settings and are not operated by the Pocket Stats developer.

7. Data Sharing

Pocket Stats does not share user data with the developer or with third parties for advertising, analytics, or other independent purposes.

When the Tournaments feature is used, the Limitless username entered by the user may be transmitted directly to Limitless services as described in Section 5. Tournament information is retrieved from Limitless and stored locally by the App.

The App does not contain advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or a developer-operated backend for collecting user data.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Because Pocket Stats does not collect or store user data on developer-operated servers, the developer does not retain a copy of the user's application data.

Users can delete their locally stored Pocket Stats data by deleting the data of the App or by uninstalling the App from their device. Android backup and restoration behavior may depend on the device and the user's system settings.

9. Children's Privacy

Pocket Stats does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users through a developer-operated service.

10. Third-Party Services

Pocket Stats does not use third-party services for advertising or analytics.

The Tournaments feature uses services provided by Limitless to verify a Limitless username and retrieve publicly available tournament information. The App may also retrieve publicly available card images from the Limitless CDN to generate decklist images locally.

Limitless is not operated by the Pocket Stats developer. Users should refer to Limitless's own privacy policy and terms for information about how Limitless handles data.

The App may also use Android and Google Play system functionality required for installation, updates, backup, and device operation. These services are provided and governed by their respective providers.

11. Security

Pocket Stats is designed to keep user-generated application data on the user's device. The developer does not operate a server that receives or stores this data.

Communications with third-party services used by the App, such as Limitless, are made directly from the user's device.

Users are responsible for maintaining the security of their device and their device or Google account credentials.

12. Third-Party Content and Affiliations

Pocket Stats is an independent application and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Limitless, The Pokémon Company, Creatures Inc., or DeNA Co., Ltd.

Pokémon and Pokémon TCG Pocket are trademarks and properties of their respective owners. Card images and other third-party content displayed by the App remain the property of their respective rights holders.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary to reflect changes to the App or applicable requirements. The date shown at the top of this page indicates when the policy was last updated.
